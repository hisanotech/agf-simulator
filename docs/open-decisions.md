# Open decisions (keep out of public fixtures)

Full customer/site requirements and CAD layouts remain outside this public repository pending publication approval.

- Each of the 8 lines has an individually editable discharge interval (minutes) on the settings screen. The initial defaults are now confirmed as theoretical equipment-capacity takt values: GWI 17.2, GWII 36.5, GWIII 36.5, GWIV 22.9, GWV 25.3, GWVI 21.5, GWVII 36.5, GWVIII 36.5 min/PL. They are not PLC history. Fractional precision beyond the supplied one-decimal values and persistence across sessions remain unresolved.
- The uploaded DXF has `$INSUNITS=0`; the user considers its model units likely mm. The display-only private preview can use an explicit mm assumption, but physical scale remains unverified until compared with a known length. The reference origin, relevant layer semantics, and unresolved INSERTs remain open. Do not derive approved mm distances or ETA from this preview.
- Per-line pickup points and georeferenced equipment coordinates.
- Physical route graph, actual turn/stop points, individual slot-to-row map, shutter-specific stopping points and transit times. The [abstract logical map](logical-map.md) records confirmed corridor relationships but is not measured physical routing. Use the [route annotation guide](route-annotation-guide.md); do not silently promote provisional paths to confirmed geometry.
- AGF turn behavior is confirmed as stop-at-turn-position → rotate in place → depart only after reaching the target heading. Turn duration uses heading change and `turnRateDegPerSec`. The user-authorized provisional initial rate is 12.1deg/s, an equivalent conversion from 0.3m/s at radius 1.424m; it is not a measured stationary yaw rate. Pickup positioning/fork initial times are 0.25/0.10min and drop-off positioning/fork initial times are 0.25/0.15min; these remain editable model assumptions. Actual turn/stop positions, measured angular rate, acceleration/deceleration, clearance and interference detection distance remain unresolved. Equal runtime traffic conditions may use request/event order with explicit `synthetic runtime tie-break` evidence; this does not establish a site-specific AGF-ID priority.
- Battery consumption baseline is now 70 percentage points per 360 minutes of driving and handling (supplier assumption relayed by the user on 2026-09-25; active-time scope explicitly user-confirmed). Idle, blocked unloading and charger queues are excluded; charging travel is included. Load/speed/distance-specific rates, auxiliary idle consumption, physical charging routes, mid-task charge interruption and pre-dispatch completion-energy reservations remain unresolved. See [AGF specification](specs/07-agf.md).
- Wrapper/labeler measured durations, conveyor positions and detailed interlocks remain unresolved. The user-confirmed capacities are 1PL at the input handoff, 5PL held on the internal conveyor and 1PL at the output handoff. Five internal positions do not mean five simultaneous wrapping processors. Transport01 retains its load/task at its PGW stop when input cannot be reserved, faces the wrapper, and waits for valid input reservation plus clear traffic resources.
- Magazine initial quantity is confirmed at 10 for all five units. Aligner initial quantity is confirmed at 10 for all five units. Each real pallet requires one empty pallet to be discharged from its mapped magazine before palletizing. The 8 production-line to 5 magazine mapping, magazine physical maximum capacity, detailed source-selection priority, and whether a production opportunity blocked at magazine quantity 0 resumes immediately after refill or waits for the next takt remain unresolved.
- Transport-type priority and preemption policy.
- Local dashboard layout, reservation dialog and CSV implementation are available for user review; final acceptance, persistence across browser reloads and actual warehouse slot coordinates remain open. See [dashboard implementation](ui-dashboard.md).
- Warehouse structure is confirmed at 802PL; east column 10 is empty and its traversability unresolved. The user-defined initial traffic direction for each west/east pair is block-side north→south and wall-side south→north. Physical aisle geometry, stopping positions, widths and clearance remain unreviewed. Storage rows remain bidirectional single-lane with no side-by-side passing.
- The upper and lower fire-shutter corridor groups remain confirmed as bidirectional, two-lane and simultaneous-passing-capable. The direction of each individual lane is unresolved and must be checked without weakening the confirmed group-level attributes.
- Normal warehouse access uses the east shutter and excludes the west shutter. The east-shutter-failure detour through the west shutter and the required palletizing entry/exit reversal are retained requirements; activation conditions, operator procedure, stops and physical route remain unresolved and non-routable.
- South of EB2: two waiting places and two distinct charging places, two charger devices, five aligners; east-main-aisle access is confirmed. Initial positions are user-confirmed as AGF1→CHARGE-PLACE1, AGF2→CHARGE-PLACE2, AGF3→HP1 and AGF4→HP2. These are initial-only assignments, not dedicated return places. Initial charging-place occupancy does not charge or occupy a charger device. Do not fix charging-place1/2 to charger1/2; use available devices only on actual charging start. Actual branch/stop geometry remains unresolved. Empty-pallet storage below the aligners is AGF-forbidden, never a route/retreat/pickup point.
- Four normal warehouse waiting places are retained: HP1, HP2, east pillar-front, west pillar-front. They are shared resources rather than AGF-dedicated places. Return priority is confirmed as HP1 → HP2 → east pillar-front → west pillar-front, selecting the first unoccupied and unreserved place. Palletizing has no normal waiting place. WS1-WS4 naming/mapping and exact physical stop geometry remain unresolved.
- Dispatch mode `area_first` now prioritizes eligible AGFs at the **destination/drop-off area** by lowest battery. Fallback when no eligible AGF is in that area and equal-battery tie handling need confirmation; the engine already implements destination-area priority; ID tie breaks and optional cross-area fallback remain explicit model assumptions.

Use an explicit scenario schema and synthetic values until confirmed. Never silently resolve these by guessing.

## 2026-10-02 共有走行領域・包装機入口待ちの追加

- PZは共有走行領域として扱い、交通制御上の北側設備レーンは東→西、南側外通路レーンは西→東。必要な明示接続点でレーン変更できる。西端／東端だけを経由する旧構造や不要な入口左迂回を維持しない。実CADのレーン境界・接続位置・設備前姿勢の寸法は未確認。
- 競合は進入前停止を基本とし、区間内の車を優先、未進入同士は積載車を優先する。排他停止点の現在占有・到着予約も確認する。表示上の重なり、車体貫通、ワープで交通を成立させない。
- 包装機入口待ちは搬送01の専用状態としてPGW前に保持し、包装機方向へ停止旋回して待つ。入力予約、正規レーンと競合領域の空き、横の追い越し完了を確認して発進する。実PGW待機停止位置と必要離隔は未確認。
- 例外追い越しは、荷受け済み搬送01の包装機入口待ち車が完全停止・発進予定なしで正規レーンを塞ぐ場合だけ。明示した反対レーン経路、変更開始・側方・復帰領域、経路上の実レーン／交差点／排他停止点を一括確認・予約する。通常対向車は優先し、一般交通待ちや充電待ちへ例外を広げない。実CADの追い越し経路や車体安全余裕は未確定で、明示計画がない場合は例外経路を生成しない。
